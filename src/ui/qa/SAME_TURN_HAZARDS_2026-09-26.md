# Same-turn hazard response — 2026-09-26

Experimental branch: `balance/same-turn-hazards-2026-09-26`, created from prior emergency branch commit `70f14f5`. Prior version and `emergency-actions-v1.html` are preserved. New preview: `public/assets/previews/emergency-actions-v2.html`.

Current-turn hazard is revealed when operation starts or the next turn starts. The existing `GameState.activeHazard` field now means the hazard active **for the turn being planned**. No GameState or action shape changed. Forecasts remain fuzzy; the exact *future* schedule is not shown. At End Turn, the resolver re-derives the hazard from the authoritative seed, applies accepted player actions, evaluates mitigation, resolves systems, and checks survival. A critical resource or temperature at this settlement ends the mission immediately. There is no extra recovery turn and Challenge cannot reach turn 11 through crisis.

Mitigation values in `src/data/emergency.ts` (experimental): qualifying preparedness reduces hazard severity to 30% of scheduled severity, a 70% reduction. Power requires at least 12 stored power after selected actions. Temperature requires a connected utility module with at least 50% thermal allocation, more than 50% integrity, and at least 50% delivered power. Radiation and micrometeoroid impact require a connected shelter providing at least 0.15 protection (one shelter at 75% integrity or higher). Communications require the existing functioning backup link. Repairs, reallocation, pause, or emergency supply may satisfy conditions during the same turn; effects use the post-action state. The first accepted emergency action is free during a current hazard turn, with normal AP cost for later emergency actions. Turns without a hazard retain normal AP costs.

The UI shows the current hazard and whether queued actions meet the mitigation condition. The settled summary, report history, replay turn frame, and Mission Control notification use the **resolved** hazard, not the newly revealed next-turn hazard. Old preview v1 remains for comparison; v2 is the recommended preview for this experiment. No current balance change has been propagated to `dev` or `main`.

Validation: typecheck, build, 45 tests including same-turn rescue, mitigation and deterministic replay. The paired offline harness `src/ui/qa/emergency-paired-same-turn.mjs` completed 40 runs, maximum turn 10, zero AP-limit rejections. With the simple threshold rescue policy, 8/20 unassisted and 11/20 assisted scenarios passed. These are narrow deterministic cases, not a player win-rate estimate. The fixture had no shelter, thermal or communications backup; targeted tests cover shelter and thermal conditions, and broader gameplay trials remain useful before adopting the values.
